Recommendation

The project is viable and should continue, at AMBER status. Cost is under control: CPI is 0.99 and most of the contingency is still available. The schedule is 5 days behind after correction. We recommend that the steering committee:

  1. Approve the revised pilot go-live of 14 Dec 2026, and avoid cutover during the Christmas shutdown by moving Sites B and C to after 4 Jan 2027 if the slip grows.
  2. Hold weekly critical-path reviews on 3.3 and 5.1 until M3.
  3. Make ISSUE-02 (pharmacy interface specification) a sponsor-level escalation to the vendor.
  4. Keep the go-live readiness gate (M3) strict: zero severity-1 defects, ≥ 99.5% migration accuracy and ≥ 95% trained. Don't trade quality for the date.

Unresolved: ISSUE-02, and clinician adoption (R02), which can't be confirmed until UAT.
